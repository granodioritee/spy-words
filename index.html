    How to play
    • Split into Red and Blue. Each team picks one spymaster; everyone else guesses.
    • Spymasters see which of the 25 words belong to Red, Blue, nobody (beige) and the one assassin (black). Guessers only see the words.
    • On your team's turn, your spymaster gives one word and a number, like OCEAN 2, meaning two words on the board link to "ocean". The clue can't be a word on the board.
    • Guessers tap a word twice to pick it. Your own color: keep going (up to the number plus one). Beige or the other team's color: your turn ends. The assassin: your team loses on the spot.
    • You can end your turn after at least one guess. The team that goes first has 9 words, the other has 8. First team to uncover all of theirs wins.
